## About

Hi, I'm Aldy, the developer behind WindSync — a free SSH terminal for Windows and Android.

I'm building WindSync to make everyday server work easier: manage SSH connections, transfer files with SFTP, monitor servers, and keep connection details in a local vault. The first public release is being prepared.

WindSync is free to use, with closed-source application code. Supporting me here is completely optional. Your support helps me spend more time on development, bug fixes, and testing across devices.

No paid unlocks and no donation required to download the app. If you like what I'm building, you can buy me a coffee or follow this page for updates.

Thank you for supporting my work!
